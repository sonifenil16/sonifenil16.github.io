About

I like making models run fast on real hardware. My path went from aerospace engineering at IIT Bombay, where I led a 50-person Hyperloop team, to embedded systems and computer vision for UAVs, to on-device LLM runtimes and hand-tuned kernels. Now I work on ML infrastructure at Google.

Most of my work sits where models meet hardware: memory layout, operator coverage, quantization, kernels, and the APIs that hide all of it from the people building on top.
